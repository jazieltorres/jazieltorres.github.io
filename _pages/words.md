Over the years, students have written things to me that made me smile, encouraged me, and reminded me why I love teaching.

This is not meant to be a collection of course evaluations or a record of teaching effectiveness. It is simply a collection of words that have stayed with me.


<div class="student-quote">
  <p class="quote-text">
    "I was very lucky to have you as our tutorial professor because your contagious energy and enthusiasm made a class that is very boring in my opinion into something interesting and fun to learn."
  </p>
  <p class="quote-source">
    — Linear Algebra & Diff. Eqs Tutorial Student
  </p>
</div>
